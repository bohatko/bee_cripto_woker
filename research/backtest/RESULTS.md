## 1. Methodology & Modeling Assumptions

### 1.1 Live Logic Exact Replication (Scenario A)
- **Signal Calculation:** 4-hour candles are resampled on UTC clock boundaries (`00:00`, `04:00`, `08:00`, `12:00`, `16:00`, `20:00`). Ratio $R = P_{\text{long}} / P_{\text{short}}$. Exponential Moving Average $\text{EMA}_{10}$ is calculated over closed 4h candles with smoothing factor $\alpha = 2 / (10 + 1) = 2/11$.
- **Entry Rules:** Evaluated on every 1-minute bar close. Entry triggered if:
  $$\text{Live Ratio } R_t > \text{EMA}_{10} \quad \text{AND} \quad \text{Last Closed 4h Ratio} \ge \text{EMA}_{10}$$
  If the pair has no open position, open immediately. Sizing: Slot Margin = $25\%$ of current **Free Margin**. Position volume $= \text{Slot Margin} \times 7.0$, split equally across long and short legs.
- **Exit Rules:** Evaluated on every 1-minute bar using bar High/Low extremes:
  - Take-Profit (TP): Net spread PnL reaches **+5.0% of margin**.
  - Stop-Loss (SL): Net spread PnL reaches **-1.5% of margin**.
  - Trend-Flip: 4h candle close drops below $\text{EMA}_{10}$.
  - Conservative intra-bar touch rule: If both TP and SL could be triggered within the same 1m bar, SL is executed.
- **Re-entry:** Immediate (no cooldown). As long as $R_t > \text{EMA}_{10}$, the bot re-enters on the next 1-minute bar.

### 1.2 Realistic Cost & Friction Model
- **Taker Fee:** $0.055\%$ per leg per side (Binance USDM VIP0 futures taker fee). Each round trip involves 4 orders (open long, open short, close long, close short) $= 0.220\%$ of total notional $= 1.54\%$ of slot margin at 7x leverage.
- **Slippage:** Base $0.030\%$ per order for major coins (`SOL`, `ADA`, `BNB`, `ETH`) and $0.050\%$ per order for altcoins (`ZEC`, `AVAX`, `ENA`, `SUI`).
- **Funding Rates:** Actual historical 8h/4h funding rates downloaded from Binance USDM. Long positions pay positive funding rates; short positions receive positive funding rates.
- **Database Gross vs Net Leak:** Tracking the difference between the gross PnL shown on the live dashboard (calculated at signal mid-prices) and the actual net exchange equity after fees, slippage, and funding.

## 6. Mathematical & Microstructural Root Cause Analysis

### Why does the live bot fail?

1. **Noise-to-Barrier Asymmetry:**
   At 7.0x leverage:
   - A -1.5% margin Stop Loss is triggered by a **-0.214% adverse move in the ratio**.
   - 1-minute crypto price noise easily fluctuates $\pm 0.3\%$ within 5 minutes.
   - The probability of hitting a 0.21% barrier before a 0.71% barrier (+5% TP) in Brownian motion with zero drift is:
     $$P(\text{Hit TP}) = \frac{0.214}{0.214 + 0.714} \approx 23.0\%$$
     In our empirical backtest, the winrate was **17.10%**.

2. **Fee & Slippage Drag:**
   - Opening and closing both legs as taker costs $0.055\% \times 4 = 0.220\%$ notional.
   - At 7x leverage, this is $0.220\% \times 7 = \mathbf{1.54\%}$ of allocated margin.
   - Adding slippage ($0.03\%-0.05\% \times 4 \times 7 = \mathbf{0.84\%-1.40\%}$ of margin), total round-trip friction is **$\sim 2.38\%$ to $2.94\%$ of margin**.
   - When the bot hits Take-Profit (+5.0% gross), it only nets $+2.06\%$ to $+2.62\%$.
   - When it hits Stop-Loss (-1.5% gross), it loses **$-3.88\%$ to $-4.44\%$ net**.
   - Expected Value per trade:
     $$E[V] = 0.171 \times (+2.62\%) + 0.829 \times (-4.44\%) = +0.448\% - 3.680\% = \mathbf{-3.232\% \text{ per trade}}$$
   - Executing 82 trades per day with a $-3.2\%$ edge causes equity to decay to zero exponentially in fewer than 20 days.

   - **Leverage:** Reduce from 7.0x to **3.0x** (or 2.0x for conservative risk profiles).

## 9. Robustness, Fragility & Sensitivity Analysis

### 9.1 Barrier-Touch Bias in Scenario A (Three Conventions)

In a 1-minute discrete bar backtest with continuous barrier triggers (+5.0% TP, -1.5% SL), intrabar PnL extremes are computed as:
$$\text{MinPnL} = (P_{\text{low}}^{\text{long}} - P_{\text{entry}}^{\text{long}}) \cdot Q_{\text{long}} + (P_{\text{entry}}^{\text{short}} - P_{\text{high}}^{\text{short}}) \cdot Q_{\text{short}}$$
$$\text{MaxPnL} = (P_{\text{high}}^{\text{long}} - P_{\text{entry}}^{\text{long}}) \cdot Q_{\text{long}} + (P_{\text{entry}}^{\text{short}} - P_{\text{low}}^{\text{short}}) \cdot Q_{\text{short}}$$
$$\text{ClosePnL} = (P_{\text{close}}^{\text{long}} - P_{\text{entry}}^{\text{long}}) \cdot Q_{\text{long}} + (P_{\text{entry}}^{\text{short}} - P_{\text{close}}^{\text{short}}) \cdot Q_{\text{short}}$$

Combining $P_{\text{low}}^{\text{long}}$ with $P_{\text{high}}^{\text{short}}$ assumes instantaneous adverse cross-leg misalignment (worst-case intrabar excursion). To test whether this assumption drives the liquidation of Scenario A, we compare three barrier conventions side by side:
1. **Pessimistic (Default):** Intrabar extremes; if both TP and SL touched in the same 1m bar, SL triggers first.
2. **TP-First:** Intrabar extremes; if both TP and SL touched in the same 1m bar, TP triggers first.
3. **Close-Only (Optimistic):** Barriers evaluated strictly on the 1-minute close prices (zero intrabar noise).

However, in the real world with fees and slippage (`A_live_costs_close_only`), total execution drag ($1.42\%$ of margin per round-trip) destroys the $+0.273\%$ edge and drives net average PnL to **$-1.150\%$ per trade**, resulting in complete capital loss ($20,000 $\to$ $0.018).
- **Plain Conclusion:** Even under the most optimistic execution convention with zero intrabar noise, the live strategy is fundamentally unviable.

### 9.2 Look-Ahead Execution Timing Verification

To confirm that Scenario C and Grid D are strictly free of look-ahead bias:
1. **Signal Availability:** When a 4-hour candle spanning $[T-4\text{h}, T)$ closes at UTC timestamp $T$ (e.g. `04:00:00`), its close ratio and $\text{EMA}_{10}$ are computed using candles closed at or before $T$.
2. **Fill Execution:** The order is evaluated at timestamp $T$ and fills at the **OPEN** of the 1-minute bar starting at $T$ ($P_{\text{open}}^{\text{long}}$, $P_{\text{open}}^{\text{short}}$) plus adverse taker/maker slippage. It does not fill at the prior candle close or at the close of bar $T$.
3. **Trend-Flip Exit:** When a 4h candle close at timestamp $T$ falls below $\text{EMA}_{10}$, the exit market order executes at the **OPEN** price of the bar at $T$ ($P_{\text{open}}^{\text{long}}$, $P_{\text{open}}^{\text{short}}$) with slippage.
4. **Conclusion:** Zero future price information is used in signals, entries, or exits.
